## Lesson Learned (Project-level)

Manual PDF→MusicXML transcription was attempted for Golden (Hal Leonard 01946654 SAB+Piano).
After 8 measures of work + 3 PDCA cycles, user identified that pitch accuracy was based on
visual inference rather than pixel-verified reading. Combined with the inherent infeasibility
of pixel-perfect engraving fidelity (proprietary fonts, engraving engine differences),
the project was redirected to **path C: motif-based new arrangement** — derivative work
inspired by Golden's harmonic motif and structure, but composed independently.

Archived metrics preserved in `.bkit/state/pdca-status.json` for retrospective analysis.
